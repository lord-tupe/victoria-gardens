Our Story

More Than Just a Venue

Victoria Gardens sits on Victoria Avenue in Blantyre, surrounded by lush greenery, with well-maintained lawns, a spacious main hall, and a warm, welcoming atmosphere.

Whether you are planning a wedding, a family day out, or just want a quiet meal in a beautiful setting, this is the place. Open seven days a week from 9 AM to 10 PM.
